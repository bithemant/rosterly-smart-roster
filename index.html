ROSTERLY GUIDE

How the roster is planned

Required coverage is created for every operating process and day. Approved leave is a hard availability constraint. Preferred weekly off days are soft: the scheduler can override one when it is the only way to keep a client process covered, and the override appears as a warning.

One employee can cover multiple processes for the same brand on a day. They cannot be placed across two brands at the same time. Where the available team cannot satisfy minimum staffing, the roster leaves the position open and explains the reason.
